Volunteer Programs

👩‍🏫

Educational Workshops Facilitator

Lead workshops in schools and community centers to educate youth about gender equality principles and practices.

Commitment: 4-6 hours/week
Location: Various locations
Training: Provided
📱

Digital Advocacy Volunteer

Help manage our social media presence and create engaging content to spread awareness about gender equality issues.

Commitment: 3-5 hours/week
Location: Remote
Skills: Social media, content creation
🤝

Mentorship Program

Mentor young women in STEM fields, business, or politics to help close the gender gap in these areas.

Commitment: 2-3 hours/week
Location: In-person or virtual
Duration: 6 months minimum
📊

Research & Data Analysis

Support our research efforts by collecting and analyzing data on gender disparities in various sectors.

Commitment: 5-10 hours/week
Location: Remote
Skills: Research, data analysis
📝

Grant Writing Volunteer

Help secure funding for our gender equality initiatives by drafting grant proposals and reports.

Commitment: Flexible
Location: Remote
Skills: Writing, research
